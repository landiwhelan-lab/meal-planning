White Miso Soup with 2 Soft-Boiled Eggs & Rice
~16 g protein (Landi) 🍲 Broth-forward

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

  • 4 large eggs, soft-boiled (7 min from boiling)
  • 600 ml chicken stock (stock cubes/bone broth concentrate + boiling water)
  • 30 g white miso paste (fridge) — dissolved in a ladle of hot stock
  • 10 ml soy sauce · a few drops sesame oil
  • 120 g cooked Japanese rice (optional — 60 g each)
  • Spring onion, sliced, to finish

Method

  1. Soft-boil eggs 7 min, cool under cold water, peel and halve.
  2. Heat stock to just below a boil. Dissolve miso in a ladle of stock, stir back in. Do not boil. Add soy and sesame oil.
  3. Serve soup in bowls, eggs and a small scoop of rice alongside. Eggs first. Top with spring onion.
Warm protein-forward start. Broth counts towards fluid — but sip, don't gulp.

Chicken Espetada with Roast Veg
~30 g protein (Landi's portion)

Proteins (×4)

  • 2 chicken espetada (from freezer) — defrost in fridge overnight

Roast Veg (×4)

  • 1 butternut, peeled and cubed
  • 3–4 carrots, cut into batons
  • Green beans (fridge), trimmed

Egg Drop Soup with Spring Onion
~18 g protein (Landi) 🍲 Broth-forward

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

  • 4 large eggs, beaten
  • 700 ml chicken stock
  • 10 ml soy sauce · 5 ml sesame oil · white pepper
  • 10 ml Maizena slurry (mix with a little cold water)
  • Spring onion, sliced · a handful baby spinach (optional)

Method

  1. Bring stock to a gentle simmer. Add soy, sesame oil, white pepper.
  2. Stir in Maizena slurry to lightly thicken (1 min).
  3. Turn heat to low. Slowly pour beaten eggs in a thin stream while stirring gently — they form silky ribbons. Add spinach to wilt.
  4. Finish with spring onion. Serve hot.
Fast, high-protein and very easy to eat. The thickened broth is gentle on the stomach.

Dinner · ~18:30
Lactose-Free Mac & Cheese
Landi: ~16 g protein with liver-cake side

Ingredients (×4)

  • 320 g macaroni (≈80 g dry per person)
  • 40 g flour · 40 g butter (or 30 ml oil) for the roux
  • 600 ml lactose-free full cream milk
  • 200 g lactose-free cheddar, grated (+ a little extra for the top)
  • 1 tsp mustard · pinch nutmeg · salt · pepper
  • For Landi's protein side: 1–2 lamb liver cakes (freezer)

Method

  1. Cook macaroni in salted water until just al dente. Drain.
  2. Melt butter, stir in flour, cook 1 min. Gradually whisk in lactose-free milk to a smooth sauce. Simmer until thickened.
  3. Off the heat, stir in most of the grated lactose-free cheddar, mustard, nutmeg, salt, pepper.
  4. Fold in the macaroni. Tip into a dish, scatter remaining cheese on top, grill/bake at 200°C until golden (~10–12 min).
  5. Landi: reheat 1–2 lamb liver cakes in the air fryer (180°C, 4–5 min). Eat the liver cake FIRST, then a small portion of mac & cheese. The rest of the family have mac & cheese only.
Why the liver cake: mac & cheese alone is high-carb and low-protein, which works against your protein-first rule and the dumping risk. The liver cake gives you a proper protein anchor and good iron. Keep the pasta portion small.

Warthog Mince Cottage Pie
~26 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 500 g warthog mince (from freezer) — defrost in fridge overnight
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, grated · 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 15 ml tomato paste · 10 ml garlic puree · 10 ml beef marinade or Worcestershire
  • 250 ml beef stock · 1 bay leaf · thyme · 10 ml Maizena slurry to thicken
  • Mash topping: 5–6 potatoes (pantry), boiled and mashed with a splash of lactose-free milk, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the mince in a dry pan, breaking it up. Drain any fat (game mince is lean).
  2. Add onion, carrot, garlic. Cook 4–5 min. Stir in tomato paste, tomatoes, marinade, stock, bay, thyme.
  3. Simmer 15–20 min, thicken with a little Maizena slurry. Season.
  4. Boil and mash the potatoes with lactose-free milk, salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon mince into a dish, top with mash, fork the surface. Bake at 200°C ~20 min until golden.
  6. Landi's portion: mostly the mince (protein first), a thin layer of mash.
Uses one game mince pack — the kudu pack stays in the freezer for next week. No protein repeats around it (mac & cheese Tue, pork Thu).

Lamb Dumpling Hotpot with Udon & Greens
~27 g protein (Landi's portion)

Proteins (×4)

  • Lamb dumplings (freezer) — top up with 1 extra bag to feed ×4 (see Shop)

Broth & Sides (×4)

  • 1.4 L chicken stock · 20 ml soy · 10 ml mirin · 10 ml ginger puree · 5 ml sesame oil
  • Spoonful white miso dissolved in the broth (optional)
  • Udon noodles (pantry) — 60 g dry per person
  • Baby spinach / pak choi · carrots · spring onion

Dipping Sauce

  • Gochujang: 10 ml gochujang · 20 ml ponzu · 5 ml rice vinegar · a few drops sesame oil
  • Or ponzu straight from the bottle

Method

  1. Bring stock to a simmer with soy, mirin, ginger, sesame oil (and miso if using).
  2. Steam the lamb dumplings in the Instant Pot (200 ml water, trivet, HIGH pressure 5 min, quick release) — or simmer gently in the broth.
  3. Cook udon separately, portion into bowls. Add greens and carrots to the broth to wilt/soften.
  4. Ladle broth over noodles, top with dumplings and spring onion. Serve dipping sauce alongside.
  5. Landi's portion: dumplings first (protein), then a small bowl of broth and a few noodles. Eat slowly.
Uses the freezer lamb dumplings. Steaming keeps fat low and the gochujang dip carries the flavour.

Beef Shin Pot Roast with Roast Potatoes, Butternut & Gravy
~32 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • Beef shin (from freezer) — defrost in fridge overnight
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots, garlic puree · 2 bay leaves · thyme
  • 1 L beef stock · 15 ml tomato paste · 10 ml Worcestershire/beef marinade
  • Potatoes (pantry) · butternut · carrots for roasting
  • Maizena slurry to thicken gravy · 5 ml olive oil

Method

  1. Instant Pot: brown the shin on Sauté. Add onion, carrot, garlic, tomato paste, bay, thyme, beef stock, marinade. Pressure cook HIGH 45–50 min, natural release. Meat should be fall-off-the-bone tender.
  2. Meanwhile roast potatoes, butternut and carrots at 200°C ~40 min until golden.
  3. Strain off the cooking liquid, skim fat, reduce on the hob and thicken with a little Maizena slurry for gravy.
  4. Shred/portion the shin. Serve with roast veg and gravy — a proper traditional Sunday lunch.
  5. Landi's portion: generous beef (protein first), a few roast potatoes and butternut, gravy. Eat slowly — this is the big meal of the day.
Uses the freezer beef shin. Bigger lunch, smaller dinner as you prefer on Sundays.
