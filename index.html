Dinner · ~18:30
Spaghetti bolognese (gluten-free pasta)
~28 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 500 g beef mince
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green pepper, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
  • 1 beef stock cube dissolved in 200 ml hot water
  • 1 tsp mixed dried herbs
  • 250 g gluten-free spaghetti
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and green pepper and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute.
  3. Turn the heat to medium-high, add the beef mince and break it up with a spoon. Cook for 6–8 minutes until fully browned with no pink left.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes, stock, and herbs. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Turn heat to low and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
  7. Meanwhile bring a pot of salted water to the boil and cook the gluten-free spaghetti per the packet time (usually 8–10 minutes), then drain.
  8. Serve the sauce over the pasta, ×4. Landi: small portion of pasta, extra sauce/mince on top (protein first).

Dinner · ~18:30
Seared duck breasts with roast butternut & onion
~24 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 duck breasts (~180 g each)
  • 600 g butternut, peeled and cubed (~2 cm)
  • 2 onions, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp fresh or dried thyme
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Toss the butternut and onion wedges with the olive oil, thyme, salt and pepper on a baking tray.
  2. Roast for 30–35 minutes, turning once halfway, until the butternut is soft and lightly caramelised.
  3. Meanwhile, pat the duck breasts dry. Score the skin in a criss-cross pattern (don't cut into the meat) and season both sides with salt.
  4. Place the duck skin-side down in a cold, dry pan, then turn the heat to medium. Cook for 6–8 minutes so the fat renders and the skin turns golden and crisp. Pour off excess fat once or twice.
  5. Flip and cook 3–4 minutes more for medium (longer if you prefer well done — the thickest part should be firm, not soft).
  6. Rest the duck on a board for 5 minutes, then slice across into strips.
  7. Serve the sliced duck ×4 with the roast butternut and onion. Landi: eat the duck first, smaller veg portion.

Breakfast · ~07:00
Miso broth with silken tofu & soft egg
~15 g protein (Landi) 🍲 Broth-forward

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

  • 500 ml water
  • 2 tbsp red or white miso paste
  • 200 g silken tofu, cubed
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 spring onion, sliced

Method

  1. Soft-boil the 2 eggs: 6 minutes in boiling water, then cool and peel. Set aside.
  2. Heat the water in a pot until steaming (not boiling hard). Turn off the heat.
  3. Spoon the miso into a ladle, lower it into the water and stir to dissolve (don't boil miso — it dulls the flavour).
  4. Gently add the cubed silken tofu and warm through for 1 minute.
  5. Divide between 2 bowls, add a halved soft egg to each, scatter with spring onion. Sip the broth between spoonfuls of tofu and egg.

Dinner · ~18:30
Oven-baked pork loin chops with mash & tomato-cucumber-avo salad
~26 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 pork loin chops
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper
  • 800 g potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp lactose-free milk + 1 tsp butter (for mash)
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • ½ cucumber, diced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • Squeeze of lemon, olive oil for the salad

Method

  1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Pat the pork chops dry, rub with the olive oil, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Place on a lined tray and bake for 22–25 minutes, until the juices run clear and the thickest part is no longer pink (internal 68–70 °C if you have a thermometer). Rest 3 minutes.
  3. While the pork bakes, boil the cubed potatoes in salted water for 15 minutes until a knife slides in easily. Drain, add the milk and butter, and mash smooth. Season.
  4. Make the salad: combine the tomato, cucumber and avocado, dress with a squeeze of lemon and a little olive oil, season.
  5. Serve each chop ×4 with mash and salad. Landi: eat the pork first, smaller mash portion.

Dinner · ~18:30
Lemon-and-herb baked chicken thighs with cauliflower & green beans
~26 g protein (Landi)
Same recipe as 16 July — it worked well. Bake 2 extra thighs if you want to shred them for Sunday's broth (keep refrigerated).

Ingredients (×4)

  • 8 chicken thighs (bone-in or boneless)
  • Juice of 1 lemon + a few lemon slices
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs (or fresh thyme/oregano)
  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 300 g green beans, trimmed
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Mix the lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, herbs, salt and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Coat the chicken thighs in the mixture and place skin-side up in a baking dish with the lemon slices.
  3. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the skin is golden and the juices run clear (internal 75 °C at the thickest part; no pink at the bone).
  4. Meanwhile, steam the cauliflower florets for 8 minutes and the green beans for 5 minutes, until tender but still bright.
  5. Serve the thighs ×4 with the cauliflower and green beans. Landi: eat the chicken first.

Dinner · ~18:30
Hake with baked potatoes & tomato-onion-avo salad
~24 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 hake fillets (~150 g each)
  • Juice of ½ lemon, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper
  • 4 medium baking potatoes
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Squeeze of lemon + olive oil for the salad

Method

  1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Prick the potatoes with a fork, rub with a little oil and salt, and bake directly on the rack for 50–60 minutes until soft inside (a knife slides through easily).

  4. Make the salad: layer the tomato, onion and avocado, dress with lemon and olive oil, season.
  5. Serve each hake fillet ×4 with a baked potato and salad. Landi: eat the fish first, smaller potato.

Batch Prep Guide — Week of 20 July

🥚 Hard-Boiled Eggs — Batch (Sunday or Monday evening)

Boil 8 eggs at once: lower into boiling water, cook 9–10 minutes for firm yolks, then cool in cold water and peel. Store peeled in a covered container in the fridge — they carry the protein in the Tue/Fri/Sat snacks, the Wed & Fri egg-mayo lunches, the Thu tofu bowl, and Sunday breakfast. Boil a fresh batch mid-week if you run low.

🫙 Yoghurt-Chia Pots (night before)

For the Mon/Wed mid-morning and Sunday breakfast, stir 1 tsp chia into each yoghurt portion the night before and refrigerate — the chia softens and thickens overnight. Grate the apple in fresh in the morning so it doesn't brown.

🍗 Extra Chicken for Sunday (optional — Thursday)

If you'd rather not buy separate chicken for Sunday's broth, bake 2 extra chicken thighs with Thursday's dinner, let them cool, and keep refrigerated in a sealed container. Shred and add to the broth on Sunday. Three days in the fridge is fine for cooked chicken kept cold.

🍖 Defrost Schedule

Move each protein from freezer to fridge the night before if it's frozen:

• Mon: beef mince — out Sunday night.
• Tue: duck breasts — out Monday night (thaw fully before searing).
• Wed: pork loin chops — out Tuesday night.
• Thu: chicken thighs — out Wednesday night.
• Fri: hake — out Thursday night (fish thaws quickly; cook the day it defrosts).
• Sun: chicken for the broth — out Saturday night (skip if using Thursday's leftovers).
