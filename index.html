Batch Prep Guide — Week of 22 June

🥚 Hard-Boiled Eggs — Batch 1 (Sunday or Monday evening)

Boil 8 eggs. Covers Mon/Wed afternoon snacks for Landi + Mom (×2 people × 2 days = 8 eggs). Store unpeeled in fridge up to 6 days.

Method

  1. Place 8 eggs in a pot, cover with cold water by 2 cm.
  2. Bring to a boil, reduce to a gentle simmer, cook 10 minutes.
  3. Transfer to iced water for 5 minutes. Store unpeeled in a sealed container in fridge.
  4. Per serving: peel 2 eggs, halve, drizzle 5 ml soy sauce.

White Miso Soup with 2 Soft-Boiled Eggs & Rice
~16 g protein (Landi)🍜 Broth-forward

Ingredients (per serving — make ×2)

  • 2 large eggs, soft-boiled (7 min from boiling)
  • 300 ml chicken stock (stock cube or bone broth concentrate + boiling water)
  • 15 g white miso paste (fridge) — dissolved in a ladle of hot stock
  • 5 ml soy sauce · 3–4 drops sesame oil
  • 60 g cooked short-grain Japanese rice (small scoop)
  • Spring onion or a few cucumber slices for colour

Method

  1. Soft-boil eggs 7 min. Cool in cold water, peel, halve, drizzle with soy.
  2. Bring stock to just below a boil. Dissolve miso in a ladle of hot stock, stir back in, add sesame oil. Do not boil after the miso goes in.
  3. Serve miso soup in a bowl with rice and eggs alongside. Eggs first — protein first.
Warm, gentle start to the week. Counts towards the fluid target — sip the broth slowly.

Warthog Mince Bolognese — Landi over Soft Polenta, Family over Pasta
~28 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 1 × 500 g warthog mince (defrost Sun evening)
  • 2 medium onions, diced · 4 cloves garlic, minced · 1 green pepper, diced
  • 3 medium tomatoes, chopped (or 1 tin chopped tomatoes) · 15 ml tomato paste if available
  • 1 beef stock cube in 100 ml hot water · 5 g dried mixed herbs (oregano, basil)
  • 5 ml olive oil · salt and pepper · 5 ml Maizena slaked in 15 ml cold water (to thicken)
  • Landi's base: 80 g soft polenta cooked in chicken stock (no butter)
  • Family base: pasta of choice (cook ~320 g dry)

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot. Brown the warthog mince in batches, breaking it up well. Set aside.
  2. Sauté onion 3–4 min, add garlic and green pepper, cook 2 min more.
  3. Return mince. Add tomatoes, tomato paste, beef stock, herbs, salt and pepper. Simmer on low 25–30 min.
  4. Stir in the slaked Maizena and simmer 2 min to thicken (warthog is very lean, so the sauce can be thin).
  5. Landi's polenta: bring 250 ml chicken stock to a simmer, whisk in 40 g dry polenta, stir 5 min until smooth. Cook the family's pasta separately.
  6. Landi's plate: 100–120 g mince sauce over 80 g soft polenta. No cheese or cream. Protein first.
Note for Mom: No cream or cheese on Landi's portion. Polenta (not pasta) for Landi keeps it gentle on her stomach — pasta/bread are off her plate. The green pepper must go in.

Bone Broth with Poached Eggs & Greens

Kudu Mince Curry on Rice
~26 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 1 × 500 g kudu mince (defrost Thu evening)
  • 2 onions, diced · 4 cloves garlic, minced · 5 ml pureed ginger
  • 1 green pepper, diced · 2 carrots, diced (adds bulk + fibre)
  • 30 ml curry paste (pantry) — adjust to taste · 5 g turmeric · 5 g ground cumin
  • 3 tomatoes chopped (or 1 tin) · 1 beef stock cube in 150 ml hot water
  • 60 ml coconut cream (pantry) — stirred in at the end for richness (low-sugar)
  • 5 ml olive oil · 5 ml Maizena slaked in 15 ml cold water (to thicken) · salt to taste
  • White rice (pantry) for the family; Landi: 60 g cooked rice (small scoop)

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot. Brown the kudu mince in batches, breaking it up. Set aside.
  2. Sauté onion 3–4 min. Add garlic, ginger, green pepper and carrot, cook 3 min.
  3. Add curry paste, turmeric and cumin, fry 1 min until fragrant.
  4. Return mince. Add tomatoes and beef stock. Simmer on low 25–30 min until carrots are tender.
  5. Stir in coconut cream and the slaked Maizena. Simmer 2–3 min to thicken. Season.
  6. Cook rice for the family. Landi's plate: 100–120 g mince curry + 60 g rice. Protein first.
Note for Mom: Kudu is very lean — don't overcook or it dries out. Coconut cream (not coconut milk) keeps it rich without added sugar. Keep Landi's rice scoop small. Watch the chilli level in the curry paste for Landi.

Steamed Lamb Dumplings with Udon Broth & Veg Sides
~28 g protein (Landi's portion)

Proteins (×4)

  • Lamb dumplings (陶老大 羊肉大葱水饺) — partial bag from freezer + 1 bought bag to make up ×4 (defrost Fri evening) — ~9.2 g protein per 100 g
  • Optional: 2 eggs to poach in the broth for extra protein

Udon Noodle Broth (×4)

  • 4 portions udon noodles (pantry) — 60 g dry per person, boiled 3–4 min, drained
  • 1.2 L chicken stock (stock cubes or bone broth concentrate)
  • 20 ml soy · 10 ml mirin · 10 ml pureed ginger · 5 ml sesame oil
  • Optional: a spoonful of white or red miso dissolved into the broth

Veg Sides

  • Cucumber and carrot batons · baby spinach or pak choi to wilt in the broth

Dipping Sauces

  • Ponzu: straight from the bottle
  • Gochujang: 10 ml gochujang · 20 ml ponzu · 5 ml rice vinegar · 3–4 drops sesame oil

Method

  4. Serve dumplings over the udon broth with dipping sauces and veg batons. Optional: poach 2 eggs in the broth for a protein boost.
  5. Landi's portion: 4–5 dumplings + small bowl udon broth with a few noodles. Protein first (dumplings). Eat slowly, chew well.
Tip: Steaming the dumplings (not pan-frying) keeps fat low — the dipping sauce carries the flavour. Gochujang sauce is the winner with lamb dumplings. Buy one extra bag of dumplings since your freezer bag is only partial.

Instant Pot Beef Shin Pot Roast with Roasted Vegetables
~30 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • Beef shin (from freezer — defrost from Wed evening)
  • 2 onions, chopped · 4 cloves garlic · 3 carrots, chunked · 2 celery sticks if available
  • 1 beef stock cube in 400 ml hot water · 15 ml tomato paste · 2 bay leaves · 5 g dried thyme
  • 5 ml Maizena slaked in 15 ml cold water (to thicken the gravy)
  • Roasted veg: butternut or sweet potato + carrots (to buy), cubed, roasted in olive oil
  • Optional small scoop rice or polenta for the family · green beans steamed

Method

  1. Sear: Season the beef shin. Use the Instant Pot sauté function to brown it on all sides in a little olive oil. Remove.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, carrot and celery 3–4 min. Stir in tomato paste 1 min.
  3. Return beef shin. Add beef stock, bay leaves and thyme. Seal lid.
  4. Pressure cook on HIGH 50–60 min. Natural release 15 min, then quick release.
  5. Meat should be fall-apart tender. Shred off the bone, discard bone, skim fat from the juices. Stir the slaked Maizena into the juices and simmer (sauté mode) 2–3 min for a glossy gravy.
