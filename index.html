Miso Soup with Soft-Boiled Egg & Rice
~16 g protein (Landi) 🍲 Broth-forward

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

  • 4 large eggs, soft-boiled (7 min), cooled and peeled
  • 600 ml chicken stock (stock cubes or bone broth concentrate + boiling water)
  • 30 g white miso paste (fridge), dissolved in a ladle of hot stock
  • 10 ml soy sauce · a few drops sesame oil
  • 120 g cooked Japanese rice (from batch — 60 g each)
  • Spring onion, sliced, to finish

Method

  1. Heat stock to just below a boil. Dissolve miso in a ladle of stock, stir back in — do not boil.
  2. Add soy and sesame oil. Serve in bowls with the eggs (halved) and a small scoop of rice alongside.
  3. Eggs first. Top with spring onion.

Duck Breast in Smoky BBQ Sauce with Roast Veg
~26 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • Duck breast portions in smoky BBQ marinade (from freezer) — defrost in fridge overnight (moved Tue night)
  • Butternut, peeled and cubed · carrots, halved lengthways
  • 5 ml olive oil · salt · pepper

Method

  1. Toss butternut and carrots in olive oil, salt and pepper. Roast at 200°C ~30–35 min until tender and golden.
  2. Pan-sear the duck breasts skin-side down 4–5 min over medium heat to render fat, flip and cook a further 3–4 min. Rest 5 min, then slice.
  3. Serve sliced duck over the roast veg, spooning over any pan juices.
  4. Landi's portion: duck first (protein first), a modest amount of veg. Eat slowly.

Kudu Mince & Sweet Potato Shepherd's Pie
~26 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 500 g kudu mince (from freezer) — defrost in fridge overnight (moved Tue night)
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 carrots, grated · 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 15 ml tomato paste · 10 ml garlic puree · 10 ml beef marinade or Worcestershire
  • 250 ml beef stock · 1 bay leaf · thyme · 10 ml Maizena slurry to thicken
  • Mash topping: 2 medium sweet potatoes, boiled and mashed with a splash of lactose-free milk, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the kudu mince in a dry pan, breaking it up. Drain any fat (game mince is lean).
  2. Add onion, carrot, garlic. Cook 4–5 min. Stir in tomato paste, tomatoes, marinade, stock, bay, thyme.
  3. Simmer 15–20 min, thicken with a little Maizena slurry. Season.
  4. Boil and mash the sweet potato with lactose-free milk, salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon mince into a dish, top with mash, fork the surface. Bake at 200°C ~20 min until golden.
  6. Landi's portion: mostly the mince (protein first), a thin layer of mash.
Uses the last freezer kudu mince pack. Different protein from Wednesday's duck and Friday's lamb.

Lamb Curry with Rice
~24 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • ~700 g lamb pieces (fridge — already fresh, no defrost needed)
  • 1 onion, sliced · 10 ml garlic puree · 10 ml ginger puree
  • 30 ml curry paste (pantry) · 200 ml coconut cream (pantry)
  • 250 ml lamb or chicken stock · 2 carrots, sliced
  • 240 g Japanese or white rice, cooked (60 g each)

Method

  1. Instant Pot: brown lamb pieces on Sauté. Add onion, garlic, ginger, cook 3 min.
  2. Stir in curry paste, coconut cream, stock and carrots. Pressure cook HIGH 25–30 min, natural release until lamb is tender.
  3. Simmer uncovered a few minutes to thicken if needed. Season.
  4. Serve over rice. Landi's portion: lamb and sauce first (protein first), a small scoop of rice.
Uses the fresh fridge lamb. Different protein from Thursday's kudu mince and Saturday's hake.

Beef Shin Pot Roast with Roast Potatoes, Butternut & Gravy
~32 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • Beef shin (from freezer) — defrost in fridge from Friday night (large cut, needs ~2 days)
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots, garlic puree · 2 bay leaves · thyme
  • 1 L beef stock · 15 ml tomato paste · 10 ml Worcestershire/beef marinade
  • Potatoes (pantry) · butternut · carrots for roasting
  • Maizena slurry to thicken gravy · 5 ml olive oil

Method

  1. Instant Pot: brown the shin on Sauté. Add onion, carrot, garlic, tomato paste, bay, thyme, beef stock, marinade. Pressure cook HIGH 45–50 min, natural release. Meat should be fall-off-the-bone tender.
  2. Meanwhile roast potatoes, butternut and carrots at 200°C ~40 min until golden.
  3. Strain off the cooking liquid, skim fat, reduce on the hob and thicken with a little Maizena slurry for gravy.
  4. Shred/portion the shin. Serve with roast veg and gravy — a proper traditional Sunday lunch.
  5. Landi's portion: generous beef (protein first), a few roast potatoes and butternut, gravy. Eat slowly — this is the big meal of the day.
Uses the freezer beef shin — wasn't needed last Sunday since you ate at friends'. Bigger lunch, smaller dinner as usual.
