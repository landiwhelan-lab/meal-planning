Dinner · ~18:30
Beef mince lasagna
~30 g protein (Landi)
Uses the freezer beef mince (defrost per Batch Prep) and the lasagna sheets already in the pantry.

Ingredients (×4)

  • 500 g beef mince (defrosted)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 beef stock cube dissolved in 100 ml hot water
  • 1 tsp mixed dried herbs
  • 8–10 lasagna sheets (from the pantry)
  • 500 ml lactose-free milk
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 100 g grated hard cheese (cheddar or parmesan)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  2. Turn the heat to medium-high, add the beef mince and break it up with a spoon. Cook 6–8 minutes until fully browned with no pink left.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2 minutes. Add the chopped tomatoes, stock and herbs, and season. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  4. Make the white sauce: melt the butter in a separate saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook 1 minute, stirring constantly. Gradually whisk in the lactose-free milk, a splash at a time, until smooth. Cook, whisking, for 4–5 minutes until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Season lightly.
  5. Heat the oven to 190 °C. In a baking dish, spread a third of the meat sauce, layer lasagna sheets on top (breaking to fit the dish), then spread a third of the white sauce over. Repeat the layers twice more, finishing with white sauce on top. Scatter the grated cheese over the surface.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbling and a knife slides easily through the pasta.
  7. Rest 5 minutes before cutting into 4 portions. Landi: smaller pasta portion, extra meat sauce on top — protein first. Take a MilkAid if the cheese topping bothers you.

Lamb dumpling & miso soup
~20 g protein (Landi) 🍲 Broth-forward
Moved from dinner this week — step-dad isn't keen on Japanese-style flavours at dinner, so this stays a Landi + Mom lunch.

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

  • 250 g frozen lamb dumplings (from the freezer bag)
  • 600 ml water
  • 2 tbsp miso paste (red or white)
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)

Method

  1. Bring the water to a simmer in a pot. Add the frozen dumplings straight in — no need to defrost first.
  2. Simmer for 6–8 minutes until the dumplings float and the filling is cooked through (cut one open to check there's no pink lamb inside).
  3. Turn off the heat. Spoon the miso into a ladle, lower it into the pot, and stir to dissolve (don't let it boil hard once the miso is in — it dulls the flavour).
  4. Ladle into 2 bowls, drizzle with sesame oil if using, and scatter with spring onion. Sip the broth between dumplings.

Dinner · ~18:30
Lamb chops with mash & green salad
~26 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 8 lamb chops (~120 g each)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried rosemary, salt, pepper
  • 800 g potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp lactose-free milk + 1 tsp butter (for the mash)
  • Handful lettuce or spinach, ½ cucumber, 1 tomato (salad)
  • Squeeze of lemon, olive oil for the salad

Method

  1. Rub the lamb chops with the olive oil, rosemary, salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a pan over medium-high heat. Sear the chops for 4–5 minutes per side, until no pink juices run and the thickest part is firm to the touch (longer for well done). Rest 3 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, boil the cubed potatoes in salted water for 15 minutes until a knife slides in easily. Drain, add the milk and butter, and mash smooth. Season.
  4. Toss the lettuce/spinach, cucumber and tomato with a squeeze of lemon and a little olive oil.
  5. Serve 2 chops each ×4 with mash and salad. Landi: eat the lamb first, smaller mash portion.

Dinner · ~18:30
Creamy chicken & mushroom pancakes
~28 g protein (Landi)
Husband's request this week. Also bake the 2 extra chicken thighs listed below alongside — cool and refrigerate them for Sunday's broth (see Batch Prep).

Ingredients (×4, plus 2 extra thighs for Sunday)

  • Crêpes: 200 g flour, 3 eggs, 400 ml lactose-free milk, 1 tbsp melted butter, pinch of salt
  • Filling: 500 g chicken breast, cut into small pieces
  • 2 extra chicken thighs (bake separately for Sunday)
  • 250 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 300 ml lactose-free cream (or lactose-free milk + 1 tbsp cornflour)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried thyme, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Make the crêpe batter: whisk the flour and a pinch of salt in a bowl. Make a well in the centre, add the eggs, and whisk in gradually, then whisk in the milk a little at a time until smooth and thin. Whisk in the melted butter. Rest 15 minutes.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and brush lightly with oil. Pour in a thin layer of batter, swirl to coat the base, cook 1–2 minutes until the edges lift, flip and cook 30 seconds more. Repeat to make 8 crêpes, stacking with baking paper between them.
  3. Season the 2 extra chicken thighs with salt and pepper and bake at 200 °C for 35–40 minutes until the juices run clear and there's no pink at the bone. Cool and refrigerate for Sunday.
  4. For the filling: heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and mushrooms, cook 5 minutes until the mushrooms release their liquid and it evaporates.
  5. Add the chicken breast pieces and cook 6–8 minutes until no pink remains and the juices run clear.
  6. Stir in the cream (or the milk mixed smooth with the cornflour first) and thyme. Simmer 4–5 minutes until thickened, season.
  7. Spoon the filling down the centre of each crêpe and roll or fold into quarters. Serve 2 filled crêpes per person, ×4. Landi: eat the filling first, smaller crêpe portion.

Dinner · ~18:30
Pan-seared hake with lemon-garlic butter, rice & green beans
~24 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 hake fillets (~150 g each)
  • 2 tbsp flour, seasoned with salt & pepper (for dusting)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 30 g butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 300 g rice
  • 300 g green beans, trimmed

Method

  1. Rinse the rice, then cook per packet instructions (roughly 300 g rice to 600 ml water: bring to the boil, cover, simmer 12–15 minutes until the water is absorbed, then rest covered for 5 minutes off the heat).
  2. Steam the green beans for 5 minutes until tender but still bright.
  3. Pat the hake fillets dry and dust lightly with the seasoned flour, shaking off the excess.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Fry the hake for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and it flakes easily with a fork. Remove to a plate.
  5. Lower the heat to medium, add the butter and garlic to the same pan, cook 30 seconds until fragrant, then stir in the lemon juice.
  6. Spoon the lemon-garlic butter over the hake. Serve each fillet ×4 with rice and green beans. Landi: eat the fish first, smaller rice portion.

Dinner · ~18:30
Seared duck breasts with roast butternut & onion
~24 g protein (Landi)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 duck breasts (~180 g each)
  • 600 g butternut, peeled and cubed (~2 cm)
  • 2 onions, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp fresh or dried thyme
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Toss the butternut and onion wedges with the olive oil, thyme, salt and pepper on a baking tray.
  2. Roast for 30–35 minutes, turning once halfway, until the butternut is soft and lightly caramelised.
  3. Meanwhile, pat the duck breasts dry. Score the skin in a criss-cross pattern (don't cut into the meat) and season both sides with salt.
  4. Place the duck skin-side down in a cold, dry pan, then turn the heat to medium. Cook for 6–8 minutes so the fat renders and the skin turns golden and crisp. Pour off excess fat once or twice.
  5. Flip and cook 3–4 minutes more for medium (longer if you prefer well done — the thickest part should be firm, not soft).
  6. Rest the duck on a board for 5 minutes, then slice across into strips.
  7. Serve the sliced duck ×4 with the roast butternut and onion. Landi: eat the duck first, smaller veg portion.

Batch Prep Guide — Week of 3 Aug

🥚 Hard-Boiled Eggs — Batch (Sunday or Monday evening)

Boil 8 eggs at once: lower into boiling water, cook 9–10 minutes for firm yolks, then cool in cold water and peel. Store peeled in a covered container in the fridge — they carry the protein in the Tue/Fri/Sat snacks, the Wed egg-mayo lunch, and Sunday breakfast. Boil a fresh batch mid-week if you run low.

🫙 Yoghurt-Chia Pots (night before)

For the Mon/Wed snacks and Sunday breakfast, stir 1 tsp chia into each yoghurt portion the night before and refrigerate — it softens and thickens overnight. Grate the apple in fresh in the morning so it doesn't brown.

🍗 Extra Chicken for Sunday (Wednesday)

Bake 2 extra chicken thighs alongside Wednesday's pancake filling (see that day's recipe), let them cool, and keep refrigerated in a sealed container. Shred and add to Sunday's broth. Three to four days in the fridge is fine for cooked chicken kept cold.
