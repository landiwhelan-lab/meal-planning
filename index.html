Miso Soup with Silken Tofu & Soft Egg
~18 g protein (Landi) 🍲 Broth-forward

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

  • 600 ml dashi (1 tsp dashi powder dissolved in 600 ml just-boiled water)
  • 30 g white miso paste
  • 150 g silken tofu, cut into small cubes
  • 4 eggs (2 each), for soft-boiling
  • 1 spring onion, thinly sliced · 5 ml soy sauce

Method

  1. Lower the eggs into gently boiling water and cook 6½ minutes exactly, then transfer to cold water, peel and halve.
  2. Heat the dashi to a bare simmer (do not boil). Turn off the heat.
  3. Ladle a little hot dashi into a bowl with the miso, whisk to a smooth paste, then stir back into the pot. Add the tofu and warm through 2 minutes.
  4. Pour into bowls, add the soft eggs and spring onion. Eggs and tofu first, then sip the broth slowly.

Beef Shin Pot Roast with Roast Potatoes, Butternut & Gravy
~30 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • Beef shin (from freezer) — move to the fridge Saturday night to defrost fully (see Batch page)
  • 2 onions, sliced · 15 ml garlic puree · 15 ml ginger puree
  • 750 ml beef stock (2 stock cubes in 750 ml water) · 1 bay leaf · thyme
  • 15 ml tomato paste · 10 ml beef marinade or Worcestershire
  • 800 g potatoes, peeled and halved · ½ butternut, peeled and cubed (from stock)
  • 15 ml olive oil · salt · pepper · 15 ml Maizena in 30 ml cold water (gravy slurry)

Method

  1. Pat the shin dry and season with salt and pepper. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté and brown the shin in 5 ml oil, ~3 minutes each side, then remove.
  2. Add the onions to the pot and sauté 4 minutes until soft. Stir in the garlic, ginger and tomato paste, cook 1 minute.
  3. Return the shin, add the stock, bay leaf, thyme and beef marinade. Seal and pressure-cook on High for 55 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, toss the potatoes and butternut in 10 ml oil, salt and pepper. Roast at 200°C for 35–40 minutes, turning once, until golden and tender.
  5. Lift out the shin and rest it. Set the pot to Sauté, stir in the Maizena slurry and simmer 2–3 minutes until the gravy thickens. Check seasoning.
  6. Pull the shin into chunks (discard bone and gristle) and return to the gravy. Serve with the roast potatoes and butternut.
  7. Landi's portion: a chunk of shin first (protein first), a small spoon of potato and butternut, plenty of gravy. Eat slowly, chew well.
Uses the freezer beef shin (originally planned for Sun 12 July, moved to Monday). Different protein from Tuesday's fishcakes.

Ingredients (×2 — Landi + Mom)

Method

Fishcakes with Mash & Pea Salad
~27 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 500 g white fish fillets (hake or haddock), bought fresh
  • 400 g potatoes, peeled and boiled (for binding the cakes)
  • 1 egg, beaten · 15 ml chopped fresh parsley · zest of ½ lemon · salt · pepper
  • 40 g cake flour or maize meal, for coating (no breadcrumbs)
  • 15 ml olive oil, for shallow-frying
  • Mash: 600 g potatoes, boiled and mashed with a splash of lactose-free milk, salt, pepper
  • Pea salad: 250 g peas (from frozen), 1 spring onion sliced, 10 ml chopped mint, 10 ml lemon juice, 5 ml olive oil

Method

  1. Poach the fish in barely simmering water 6–8 minutes until it flakes easily, then drain well and flake, removing any bones.
  2. Boil the 400 g potatoes until soft, drain, mash and cool slightly. Mix with the flaked fish, beaten egg, parsley, lemon zest, salt and pepper.
  3. Shape into 8 patties, dust each lightly in flour or maize meal. Chill 10 minutes to firm up.
  4. Shallow-fry in the oil over medium heat 3–4 minutes per side until golden and hot through.
  5. Boil and mash the 600 g potatoes with milk, salt and pepper. Blanch the peas 2 minutes, drain, then toss with spring onion, mint, lemon juice and oil.
  6. Landi's portion: one to two fishcakes first (protein first), a small spoon of mash, a helping of pea salad. Chew well.
Bound with potato and egg, coated in flour/maize meal instead of breadcrumbs. Different protein from Monday's beef and Wednesday's kudu.

Kudu Frikkadelle (Meatballs) in Tomato Sauce with Rice
~28 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 500 g kudu mince (from freezer) — move to the fridge Tuesday night to defrost (see Batch page)
  • 1 onion, finely grated · 1 egg · 30 g oats or maize meal (binder, no bread) · 5 ml ground coriander · salt · pepper
  • Sauce: 2 cans chopped tomatoes · 1 onion, diced · 10 ml garlic puree · 15 ml tomato paste · 5 ml dried origanum · 250 ml beef stock · 1 bay leaf
  • 10 ml olive oil · 300 g white rice, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the kudu mince with the grated onion, egg, oats, coriander, salt and pepper. Roll into ~16 meatballs.
  2. Brown the meatballs in 5 ml oil over medium heat, ~2 minutes each side, then set aside (they finish in the sauce).
  3. In the same pan, sauté the diced onion 4 minutes, add garlic and tomato paste, cook 1 minute. Add the chopped tomatoes, origanum, stock and bay leaf.
  4. Simmer the sauce 10 minutes, then return the meatballs and simmer gently a further 15 minutes until cooked through (cut one open to check — no pink inside). Season.
  5. Cook the rice according to the pack (or Instant Pot: 300 g rice + 360 ml water, High 4 minutes, natural release 10 minutes).
  6. Landi's portion: 3–4 meatballs first (protein first), a small spoon of rice, plenty of sauce. Eat slowly.

Lemon-and-Herb Baked Chicken Thighs with Cauliflower & Green Beans
~32 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 8 boneless chicken thighs (bought fresh; 2 portions set aside earlier for Tue/Sat lunches)
  • 30 ml lemon juice · 10 ml olive oil · 10 ml garlic puree · 10 ml dried mixed herbs · salt · pepper
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets · 250 g green beans, trimmed
  • 5 ml olive oil for the veg

Method

  4. Steam the green beans 4–5 minutes until just tender.
  5. Landi's portion: one thigh first (protein first), a small helping of cauliflower and beans. Chew well.

Oven-Baked Pork Loin Chops with Mash & Broccoli
~30 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 pork loin chops (lean, fat trimmed), bought fresh
  • 10 ml olive oil · 10 ml garlic puree · 5 ml dried thyme · salt · pepper
  • Mash: 600 g potatoes, boiled and mashed with a splash of lactose-free milk, salt, pepper

Method

  5. Landi's portion: chop first (protein first), a small spoon of mash, broccoli, a little gravy. Chew well.

Seared Duck Breasts with Roast Cauliflower & Butternut
~28 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 4 duck breasts, bought fresh · salt · pepper
  • 1 head cauliflower, in florets · ½ butternut, cubed (from stock)
  • 10 ml olive oil · 5 ml dried thyme
  • Optional pan sauce: 100 ml chicken stock + 5 ml soy, reduced

Method

  1. Toss the cauliflower and butternut in oil, thyme, salt and pepper. Roast at 200°C for 30–35 minutes until tender and golden.
  2. Score the duck skin in a criss-cross, season with salt. Place skin-side down in a cold dry pan, then turn the heat to medium and render 6–8 minutes until the skin is crisp and golden.
  3. Flip and cook a further 3–4 minutes for blushing-pink, or 5–6 minutes for more well done. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
  4. Optional: pour off the fat, add stock and soy to the pan and reduce 2 minutes for a light sauce.
  5. Landi's portion: sliced duck first (protein first), a small helping of roast veg, a little sauce. Eat slowly.

Roast Lamb with Cauliflower & Roast Veg
~34 g protein (Landi's portion)

Ingredients (×4)

  • 1.3–1.5 kg lamb leg or shoulder, bought fresh
  • 15 ml olive oil · 15 ml garlic puree · sprigs of rosemary · salt · pepper
  • 1 head cauliflower, in florets · ½ butternut, cubed (from stock) · 600 g potatoes, halved
  • Gravy: 300 ml lamb or beef stock · 10 ml Maizena in 20 ml cold water

Method

  1. Rub the lamb with oil, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper. Place in a roasting tray.
  2. Roast at 180°C: about 25 minutes per 500 g for pink, or 30 minutes per 500 g for well done (roughly 1½–2 hours). Rest, covered, 15 minutes before carving.
  3. Toss the potatoes, cauliflower and butternut in a little oil and salt; roast in the oven for the last 40 minutes until golden, turning once.
  4. For the gravy, skim the tray juices, add the stock on the hob, stir in the Maizena slurry and simmer 2–3 minutes until thickened.
  5. Landi's portion: sliced lamb first (protein first), a small helping of roast veg, gravy. Chew very well.
